Net session

Pad up

Just you, a net, and a bowler who wants your stumps. Every run counts until you're out, then it's back to zero.

You hold the bat. The camera is behind you and the ball comes at you. Your mouse (or finger) is the bat: move it into the ball's path.
Hold it still and the ball dies on a dead bat. Swipe through the ball as it arrives to hit it: the faster the swipe, the harder it goes. The swipe's direction steers it: sideways for direction, up to loft it, down to keep it on the ground.
Timing turns the face: early and it goes up (or gets dragged round), late and it goes into the ground (or squirts the other way). Middle of the bat = sweet; a thick edge squirts square, a thin one carries to the keeper and slips; miss it and it's on to your stumps. To leave, take the bat out of the way.
Cross bat: hold Space or the right mouse button (touch: drag the stance pad inward) and the bat turns horizontal: swipe sideways to pull, cut, hook or sweep. A straight bat only reaches about chest height, so the short stuff needs the cross bat (or a duck).
Feet: hold W to stride forward, S to go back (touch: drag the stance pad up or down). A tap is a small step, a hold a full stride; you can only press before it's bowled. Forward to the full ones, back to the short ones, and against spin go right forward or right back: the right foot lets you swing freely, the wrong one caps your swing.
1 · READ the bowler's hand (the bubble at release shows the grip) and the flight of the ball. Full or short?
2 · COMMIT your feet: W front foot, S back foot. It locks on the first press. Commit early and it's worth more; commit late and you're cramped. Against spin, E dances down the track.
3 · EXECUTE aim with the mouse around the batter (move right, hit right). Click to hit along the ground, right-click to go aerial, Space to defend. Don't click and you leave it.
Timing is judged in milliseconds. Front foot means you swing earlier than on the back foot. After every ball you're told exactly what went right or wrong. C switches camera.

Mark your run-up

Bowling is rhythm. Hit each footfall, hold on the last beat and let it go at the top. The batter's score resets every time you knock him over.

1 · PLAN put the marker on the pitch with the mouse (line and length), pick a variation with 1–5.
2 · RUN IN press Space or click to start. A beat lane appears at the bottom: after a "1, 2" count-in, tap (Space / click) as each gold footprint reaches the ring. You'll hear a click on every beat. Good rhythm = more pace, less scatter.
3 · HOLD & LET GO the green bar is the jump: press and hold as it reaches the ring, and let go on the white LET GO line. Early = fuller, late = shorter. Pace bowlers who hold late overstep: no-ball.
The batter learns. Bowl the same ball and he'll line you up. Set him up with a few, then change it.
