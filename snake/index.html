❓How to play Snakey 4D
×
Thinking in 4D

Every cell has four coordinates: X Y Z W. Each small board is a flat X–Y slice, and the boards are laid out by Z (across) and W (down).

←↑↓→ or WASD slide inside a board. JL hop one board sideways, IK hop one board up/down. You land on the same square of the neighbouring board. In 4D those squares are touching, so a hop is just one step.

The edges of every board, and the edge of the whole grid, are the walls of hyperspace (unless Walls is set to Wrap).

Tip: the dashed ring in your board marks where the food sits in its board. Line up with the ring, then hop across. The 4D Compass shows how far away the food is on each axis and which key closes the gap.

Tesseract view (V) is the same world projected from 4D down to your screen. Drag to spin it; Shift-drag (or right-drag) spins it through the 4th dimension.
