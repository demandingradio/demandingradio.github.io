THE WORLD & THE FACTIONS

The world is infinite. Drag the map in any direction and new lands unfold — rolling plains, jagged mountains, still lakes, and the deep blue boundary of the open sea. Three rival warlords share this realm with you, each carving an empire out of unclaimed neutral ground. Only one of you will rule what remains.

THE FOUR WARLORDS

YOU · The Player. Strategy is yours. You begin with a seven-hex capital and a small standing army. From this small kingdom you must do what your enemies cannot — choose.
CRIMSON · The Expansionist. Sprawls in every direction. Acts every 1.8 seconds, up to three moves at once, and will throw a stack at any target it can plausibly win. Crimson is everywhere — and thinnest where you need to look.
VERDANT · The Balanced. Methodical and patient. Acts once every 3.5 seconds. Prefers stable borders and quietly accumulates strength. Outpaces nothing but outlasts almost everything.
VIOLET · The Strategist. Tactical, fast, and frightening. Acts every 1.4 seconds — but only attacks when victory is certain. Stops at fourteen hexes and turtles behind mountains and shorelines. Do not let her finish her wall.

VICTORY & DEFEAT

Capture the crowned hex at the centre of an enemy capital to eliminate that faction. Lose yours, and you fall.

MARCHES

Selecting your hex and then clicking a destination dispatches half your troops along the shortest path through known passable land. When the column enters an enemy or neutral hex it gives battle. If it prevails it leaves a single garrison troop and presses on with the survivors. If it falls, the defenders gain that ground.

Movement is timed. A march steps once every 2.2 seconds across plains and once every 5.7 seconds through mountain country. Where you have built roads on both ends of a step the time drops to around 0.9 seconds. Water always blocks.

RALLY POINTS & MULTI-SELECT

Rallies turn a hex into a self-managing supply line. Press R, click a source, click a destination — every time that hex hits 10 troops it sends half toward the target. A dashed gold line shows the route. Clear a rally by clicking its source again in rally mode (no refund).

Multi-select handles the bulk-movement case. Hold Shift and drag across your hexes to paint a selection — every hex you touch joins. Then click any target and they all march at once. Press Esc to drop the selection.

STRATEGY & FEATURES

TERRAIN

Plains · +1 troop per tick, normal march speed.
Mountains · +1 troop every 5 ticks, 2.6× slower, +35% defender.
Lakes & Oceans · impassable. Use them as natural walls.

DEVELOPMENT (LEVELS 1 – 10)

A held hex levels up roughly every 30 seconds. Level 10 produces five times what a Level 1 hex does. Captured hexes reset to Level 1 and lose any improvement.

CAPITALS & GOLD

A capital is a seven-hex cluster with a crowned centre. Capital hexes earn +1 production and defend at +50%. Roughly one in twenty-eight passable hexes is a gold mine — owning one earns gold per tick that can be spent on improvements.

UPKEEP & FOG

Armies above an average of 25 troops per hex begin to drain. Each 10 troops of excess costs 1 troop per tick. With Fog of War on, only your vision is yours to plan in — towers extend sight, and capitals see two hexes in every direction.
